How to play

Guess words in the hidden article in English. A word’s base form also reveals its inflected forms. A guess with a similar meaning appears on the black bar.

Hints on the bars are blue for distant meanings and red for close ones. Hover over or click a bar to see the word’s length and similarity.

In the guess history, black means exact, orange means close and blue means distant. The number shows how many words matched.

Use the game ID to restart the same game. Save or share its link to play the same article revision later.

In multiplayer, players reveal the same article together. Choose multiplayer and share an invite link with a friend. They send a response back to the host. You can invite more players once connected.
